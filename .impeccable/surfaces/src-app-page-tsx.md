# Surface brief: homepage (single-page portfolio)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Bulgarian business owners with an existing, slow or dated site/store. Action: send an inquiry (contact form or email). Proof: the Omekotitel.bg case only; no stats, scores, work history, or blog. Constraints: BG/EN parity, light/dark, ⌘K, Web3Forms, existing portrait kept as is.

Memorable moment: your site's address typed into the offer's Получател field carbon-copies into the request form, and sending lands a violet ПОЛУЧЕНО stamp.

Removed from the incumbent: tech marquee, skill cards, technical-score meters, project filter (one project, empty "Blog" category), background glow/grid.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one Bulgarian pre-printed business pad (кочан): an offer, a handover protocol, a supplier record, and a request form, each a sheet filled in by hand. It refuses the dark dev portfolio of glow cards, counters, and tech marquees.

OWN-WORLD: Carbonless paper sheets (cool white original, pink copy, canary copy) printed with a green form grid, condensed caps labels, and a red numbering-machine serial. Every entry is in carbon violet at one weight, and a round violet rubber stamp marks finished things. Checkboxes are drawn as boxes marked with a pen X. Dark theme is the КОПИЕ: a carbon-violet sheet with pale impressions. Type is Sofia Sans (Lettersoup, Bulgarian Cyrillic): Extra Condensed for the print, Sofia Sans for the entries.

STORY: The visitor sees an offer addressed to their business: "I make your existing site faster and better, without starting over." The Omekotitel handover protocol is the proof. They write their site address and send the request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-width white sheet. Top row: ОФЕРТА in large green condensed caps at left, the red serial № 0000000001 and today's date at right. Below it, the Предмет cell spans the sheet and holds the headline in violet at display scale (about 5rem, three lines max). Bottom band, two cells: Доставчик (portrait clipped at an angle, name, role, 5+ years lede) and Получател (a live "your site" field with the primary button Изпратете запитване). The round stamp lands across the portrait's corner, ID-document style, most of it on paper.

FORM: Invoice book, candidate 7 of 7; seed e794b777.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION + MOTION: Carbon transfer from the Получател field to the Заявка site field; the stamp lands once (scale and blur settle, ~380ms, ease-out) on first view and again on successful send. The perforation between sheets is the only divider.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
